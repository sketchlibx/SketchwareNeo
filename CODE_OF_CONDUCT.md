# Code of Conduct

We want Sketchware Neo to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Please:
- Be respectful and considerate of different opinions, backgrounds, and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback professionally.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and community.

The following is not acceptable:
- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discrimination.
- Sexualized comments, content, or unwanted advances.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Moderation

Maintainers and community leaders may remove, edit, or reject contributions or discussions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also take appropriate corrective action, including warnings or temporary or permanent bans.

Moderation decisions should be fair and proportionate to the situation. When appropriate, the reason for a decision will be explained.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all official Sketchware Neo community spaces and to anyone representing the project in public, including through official accounts, events, or communication channels.

## Reporting

Report abusive or inappropriate behavior through [sketchlib.in](https://sketchlib.in), GitHub's reporting tools, or the appropriate project channel.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and as promptly as practical. Community leaders should respect the privacy and security of everyone involved.

## Enforcement

Depending on the situation, actions may include:

1. **Correction** — For minor or first-time issues, a private warning and clarification may be given.
2. **Warning** — Continued or more serious behavior may result in restrictions on interaction with the community.
3. **Temporary ban** — Serious or repeated violations may lead to a temporary ban from community spaces.
4. **Permanent ban** — Persistent harassment, serious misconduct, or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban.

## Contributing

To contribute:

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a feature branch from `main`.
3. Make focused changes and write clear commit messages.
4. Test your changes on a real device or emulator.
5. Open a pull request and explain what changed and why.

Useful contributions include bug fixes, performance work, UI/UX improvements, editor and block-system features, compiler/build improvements, documentation, and testing.

For larger ideas or changes that may affect the project direction, start a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/sketchlibx/SketchwareNeo/discussions).
